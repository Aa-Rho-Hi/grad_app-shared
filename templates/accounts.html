How someone gets a login

  1. They open the sign-in page, click Create account, and choose their own username and password.
  2. Their request appears above. You approve it and say what they are.
  3. For a professor, pick them from the list — or someone not on the list yet and type their name, which adds them to the faculty list and links the account in one go.

You never set anyone else's password — that's deliberate. A password you type on a colleague's behalf then exists in your head and in whatever you sent it over, and no amount of hashing on this end fixes that. For a genuine out-of-band case there's python -m src.users add.
